Research checked October 14, 2026. These are independent candidate listings; maintainers have not been represented as Commons members. Replacement mappings are recommendations for specific workflows, not claims of full parity.

Suggested index structure

One project listing can appear under several replacement pages. Keep maturity, dated maintenance evidence and contribution requests as separate fields. Add license, platform, hosting and migration filters.
